MRP Automatic Byproduct Cost Share

Optimized for Odoo 19 | Precision Byproduct Accounting

In standard Odoo, byproduct cost share must be entered manually. This module automates the entire process by calculating cost allocation **automatically** based on the product quantities and their specific Unit of Measure (UoM).

Key Features

100% Automated

Calculates cost distribution instantly. No manual data entry required, eliminating errors.

UoM Based

Uses a logical ratio of quantity produced and its specific Unit of Measure (UoM) to ensure a fair and proportionate cost distribution.

Real-time Precision

Total cost of production is always distributed across all outputs without rounding errors ($Main Product + Byproducts = 100%).
